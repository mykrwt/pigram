Quiet by design. Connected by choice.

Pigram is an independent, open-source Android patch for Instagram. Keep the people, photographs and private conversations you came for. Make recommendations and other distractions optional.

Explore releases View the source Build from source

Keep what connects

Direct messages, stories, profiles, search and the home feed remain part of the underlying Instagram app.

Choose what you see

Control feed visibility, following-feed mode, suggested accounts, Reels, Explore and ads through available patch settings.

Private by respect

Pigram does not provide its own messaging service or claim end-to-end encryption. Chats remain subject to Instagram's systems and controls.

Pigram modifies the official Instagram Android app and is not affiliated with Instagram or Meta. Compatibility depends on Instagram's app and service; direct-message support is inherited from Instagram and is not independently implemented or verified by Pigram.
